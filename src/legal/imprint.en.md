## What this application is

filahub keeps track of a 3D-printing filament stock: spools, spool types and
storage boxes with their tare weights, weigh-ins with automatic
remaining-quantity calculation, and a shared catalogue of manufacturers and
spools.

The application is not operated commercially. Nothing is sold, no advertising is
served, and no data is monetised.
