title: Log what a print used, without the scale
---

## Log usage straight from the slicer

Until now the only way to lower a material's remaining amount was to put it on
the scale. That is the most reliable way, and it stays. But after a print you
usually know what it took: the slicer told you. So you can now type that number
in, and the remaining amount drops right away.

You will find **Log usage** next to **Weigh** everywhere weighing is: in the
sidebar, on every material in the overview, on the material page, and in the
quick search. Pick the material, enter the grams, add a note if you like.

The material page shows what has been used since the last weigh-in, and its
history now lists weigh-ins and usage together, newest first, with the amount
left after each entry. So the question "is there enough left for the next one"
is answered on the page, not in your head.

## The scale still has the last word

Logged usage is a running estimate, and the next weigh-in replaces it. Entries
made before that weigh-in stay in the history, but they no longer count against
the amount – the measurement does.

If you log more than the app thinks is left, it tells you so before you save,
and the remaining amount shows 0 g rather than a negative number. That is
usually a spool that ran out, not a mistake, so nothing stops you.

Deleting a usage entry works like deleting a weigh-in: your own entry within a
quarter of an hour, older ones only if you may also edit material. Friends who
can see your stock see the reduced amount – never the entries themselves.
