---
date: 2026-08-11
title: Revoking access actually revokes it
---

The three releases that turned a filament shelf into material stores brought a
few things that were not right. Most of what follows is small. The first section
is not.

## What leaves your account

- **A friend code no longer hands out a Telegram name.** A code is meant to be
  passed around — but until now, having someone's code was enough to learn their
  Telegram username before either side had accepted anything. The name goes out
  once you are friends, and not before.
- **Withdrawing access withdraws the open requests with it.** If a friend had
  asked to borrow something and you then stopped sharing that store — or ended
  the friendship — their request stayed open. Accepting it afterwards sent the
  material's name to someone who could no longer see it. Those requests now end
  with the access, and accepting one checks the sharing again first.
- **Search-only stays search-only.** Searching over and over was a way to walk
  through a friend's whole store one term at a time, which is precisely what
  "search only" is supposed to prevent. How often the search can be repeated is
  now capped, far above anything normal use reaches.
- **Only you can clear a request you declined.** The other side could remove
  your "no" and ask again immediately.
- **Counts that were not yours.** Deleting a container type or drybox that is
  still in use tells you how many materials still hold it — and that number was
  taken across every account, not just yours. Both now count your own material,
  and an item that is not yours is simply not found instead of answering with its
  name.
- **Your data download** lists sharing you received the way the app shows it:
  one level per person. It used to spell out an entry per store, which said more
  about how someone keeps their stock than they had shared with you.

## The overview

- **A brand new account** was invited to add a material, which cannot work
  before there is a store to put it in. That state now says so and points at the
  stores.
- **Just after opening**, the overview could briefly list material from your
  other stores and count it into the totals above.
- **"No materials yet"** no longer flashes up on every cold start before your
  stock arrives.
- **The identifier field searches every store again.** Whoever reads a code off
  a spool has no idea which store is selected in the app.

## Stores and material

- **Bulk import asks where the items go.** Every position used to land in the
  selected store without saying so.
- **Adding a material** right after opening the app could leave the store empty
  and then refuse to save with "create a store first" — while you plainly had
  stores.
- **Two stores with the same name** are answered in words instead of a raw error
  message.
- **Deleting a store** and adding material to it at the same moment can no
  longer leave that material behind in a store that is gone.
- **The lending request dialog** stopped closing itself mid-typing when the
  search results behind it refreshed.
- **The store page** opens quicker: it no longer reads your whole stock to show a
  handful of numbers.

## The container catalogue

- **The catalogue entries know their shape now.** Containers that suit your
  store's material are meant to sort to the top — for every entry shipped with
  the app that did nothing, because none of them said whether they are a spool, a
  bottle or a pail. They all do, and existing installations get it filled in.
- **Copying a catalogue entry** into your own containers kept everything except
  its shape, so every copied bottle turned into a spool.
- **A heavy container with a small fill** can be suggested again. The last
  release dropped the rule that a container must weigh less than its contents —
  exactly for cartridges and steel pails — but two dialogues carried on
  enforcing it, which left the containers the change was for unreachable.
- **In English**, 71 catalogue entries said "container" where they mean "spool".
  The names are back to what the manufacturers call them.
- **"Other" no longer claims to fit everything.** A catalogue container whose
  shape is "Other" was sorted to the top for every material — resin, powder,
  filament alike — as if being a catch-all were evidence of fitting. It is the
  opposite: it says none of the five shapes apply. Such an entry now sits with
  the rest of the list instead of under "Fits resin", and it is not hidden or
  excluded anywhere.

## Smaller things

- Filament diameters read "1.75 mm" in English instead of "1,75 mm".
- The tare breakdown under the first weigh-in still called the container a
  "Rolle", and stayed German no matter which language you use.
- Roughly one friend code in a thousand could not be typed in at all.
- The import assistant's German half asked for the weight of the "spool" rather
  than the container.
