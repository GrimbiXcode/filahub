## No more deleting and re-adding

If you had put filahub on your home screen, a new release was a nuisance: the
app kept showing the old version. On the iPhone the only way out was to
delete it and add it again from the browser.

That is fixed. The app now checks for a new version every time you open it or
bring it back to the front, and reloads itself when there is one. If you are
in the middle of something – a dialog is open or you are typing – it does not
interrupt you: it shows a note with a **Reload** button instead, and tries
again the next time you come back to it.

One last time you may have to do it the old way: an app that was installed
before this version does not know how to check yet. If it keeps showing an
old version after your instance has been updated, remove it from the home
screen and add it again once – from then on it keeps itself current.
