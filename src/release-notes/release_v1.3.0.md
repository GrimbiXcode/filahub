## The Polymaker entry answers to both names

Polymaker renamed its PLA lines at the end of 2024: PolyTerra PLA became
Panchroma Matte, PolyTerra PLA+ became Satin, and the effect colours from
PolyLite became Panchroma Galaxy and Starlight. By Polymaker's own account
nothing changed but the name — same formula, same spool.

So the catalogue keeps one entry rather than two, and it now carries both
names: **Panchroma (formerly PolyTerra PLA)**. A spool bought last year and one
bought today lead to the same place, which is the point.

Two sizes were missing from it as well: the **3 kg cardboard spool** (425 g)
and the **5 kg plastic spool** (819 g).

## R3D

R3D is in the catalogue now, with its 1 kg spool at 127 g and the 3 kg spool at
320 g, both plastic.

Its cardboard spool is deliberately absent. The one database that lists it
gives two different figures for it, and no other source knows it at all — and a
starting value that is wrong is worse than one that is missing. If you own one,
weigh it and send a suggestion; that is what the suggestion queue is for.

## Nothing you changed is touched

As always, the catalogue update leaves your own edits alone.
