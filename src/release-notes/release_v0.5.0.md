## Shared spool catalogue

Instead of typing the tare weight of every spool yourself, you can now pick a
spool from a shared catalogue of manufacturers, series and versions. It ships
with Polymaker, Prusament, Bambu Lab and eSUN.

- Missing a spool? Propose it from within the app — once it has been reviewed,
  everyone benefits from the addition.
- Spools you never use can be hidden so they stay out of your way.

## Bulk import via an AI prompt

Adding a large delivery one spool at a time is tedious. The new import page
generates a prompt you can paste into any chat assistant together with a photo
or an order confirmation. Paste the answer back and review every material
before it is saved.
