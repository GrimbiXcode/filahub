## Currency and regional format per account

Prices are no longer tied to the Euro. In the settings you can choose your
display currency and the format used for numbers, weights and dates.

- Existing prices are **not** converted — only the label next to them changes.
- The regional format defaults to your browser's setting, so dates and decimal
  separators look the way you expect without configuring anything.
- Both settings belong to your account, not to the device, so they follow you
  from your phone to your desktop.

A live preview in the settings shows how a date, a weight and a price will look
before you commit to a choice.
