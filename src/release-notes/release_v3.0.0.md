date: 2026-09-16
title: A new look, built around the spool
---

## The overview is a shelf now

The overview used to be four numbers and a table. It is now a shelf: every
material is drawn as a spool, the ring around it is the fill level in the
usual colours, the core is its colour and finish. Spools stand on one shelf per
drybox, materials without a box on the last one. Three tiles sit above the
shelf: how much is left in total, with a bar that shows each material at its
share and in its own colour; which materials are running low; and what the
stock is still worth.

On a wide screen, tapping a spool opens its detail right beside the shelf –
remaining amount, the tare, the value, weigh and log buttons, and a curve of
its history. Below that width a tap opens the material page as before.

The table has not gone anywhere: **List** is one click away next to **Shelf**,
with the same columns, sorting and column settings as before. The app
remembers which of the two you last used.

## Weigh from any page

The identifier field – type `F01`, press enter, weigh – now sits in the header
on every page, together with the area and store switchers and the **Weigh**
and **Log usage** buttons. The sidebar has become a slim rail of icons; the
labels appear on hover, and the rail expands with a click on the logo if you
prefer the wide version.

## A trend for every material

The material page and the detail beside the shelf now show how fast a
material is being used – grams per week over the last three months – and how
many more weeks it should last at that pace. It is an estimate for the eye,
drawn from your weigh-ins and logged usage; the remaining amount itself still
comes from the scale. The history is drawn as a curve as well, with weigh-ins
marked as points.

## New typeface, new colours

Text is set in Manrope, every number in JetBrains Mono, so grams, percentages,
prices and dates line up and read at a glance. The dark theme has been
redrawn first this time – cool greys with a hint of blue – and the light theme
follows the same shapes.
