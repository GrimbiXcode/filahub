## Pick your columns

The material overview has always decided for you which columns to show. On a
laptop the manufacturer and the container were simply gone, and there was
nothing you could do about it — even if the manufacturer is the one thing you
look at every time.

There is now a **Columns** button above the table. It lists every column you can
turn off: identifier, type, manufacturer, remaining, container and box, price
and purchase date. Tick what you want to see, untick what you never look at. The
material itself and the weighing button stay — a table of prices without a name
is a riddle, and the button is the shortest path to the scale.

Two things worth knowing:

- **Your choice follows your account**, not the browser you made it in. Set the
  overview up once and the next device shows it the same way. This is unlike the
  light and dark theme, which stays with the device on purpose.
- **Narrow windows still drop columns by themselves.** Your selection hides
  columns on top of that; it does not force one onto a screen too narrow to hold
  it. A column you switched on can therefore still be missing on a small
  laptop — turn some others off and it has room again.

If you change nothing, the overview looks exactly as it did before.
