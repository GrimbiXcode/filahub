## Keep track of what you printed

The new **“Prints”** page in the navigation is a history of your prints. Each
entry has a title, the date, whether it worked, how long it took and on which
printer – and, most importantly, which materials it used and how much.

Add links to the model on Printables, MakerWorld or wherever you found it,
tags such as “gift” or “calibration”, and notes with simple formatting for
everything you want to remember next time: the slicer profile, what went
wrong, what you would change.

## Log a print and its usage in one step

When you pick a spool and enter the grams, logging the print also logs the
usage – the remaining quantity drops, just like with **“Log usage”**. A print
can use several materials at once, for multi-colour prints. If you do not
know which spool it came from, pick just the material: the print notes it
without changing any spool.

The quickest way stays the usage dialog: switch on **“Save as a print”**,
give it a title and, if you like, a link to the model.

Editing a print later moves its usage along if you change the material, the
grams or the date. When you delete a print, you decide whether the used
amounts go back onto the spool.

## Find it again

Search the history by title, notes, tags, links or material, and filter by
status, material, printer, tag and date. Every material and every spool now
shows its most recent prints on its page, with a link to all of them, and the
quick search (Ctrl/⌘ + K) finds prints by their title.

Your print history is private: friends you share a store with do not see it.
In an organization, everyone who can weigh can log prints.
