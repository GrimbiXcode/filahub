## Limits you will most likely never meet

An instance of filahub is only as pleasant as the people on it. To keep a single
script from filling one up, there are now upper bounds on how much one account
can create: materials in a store, weigh-ins on a material, your own colours,
finishes, container types and dryboxes, and how many catalogue suggestions you
can send in a day.

The numbers are set far above what a person reaches. A store holds a thousand
materials — and you can have five stores. A spool takes a thousand weigh-ins
before it is full. If you ever do run into one of these, the message says which
limit it was and what to do about it, and the developers would like to hear from
you: a bound that a real user meets is set wrong.

## Blocked accounts keep their data, and can ask

filahub can now block an account. It is a step taken against someone who is
causing harm, and it is deliberately not the end of the road.

A blocked account keeps its entire stock. Nothing is deleted, nothing is handed
to anyone else. Signing in still works, and what you land on is a page that says
plainly that the account is blocked and why.

From that page you can do three things. You can **download all your data**. You
can **delete your account**. And you can **ask for the block to be lifted**,
saying in your own words what happened — the answer comes back to the same page,
and as a message from the bot.

The first two are not a courtesy. Your right to a copy of your data and your
right to have it erased do not depend on good behaviour, and a block that took
them away would be worth less than the trouble it saves. So it does not take
them away.
