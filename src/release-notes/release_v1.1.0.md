## Pick your language

The interface is now available in **English and German**. You choose in the
settings, right at the top.

The language belongs to your account, not to the browser, so it follows you
from your phone to your desktop. Number, weight and date formats stay a
separate setting underneath — an English interface does not force American
dates on you.

Left on automatic, filahub follows your browser.

## The spool catalogue is bilingual too

Descriptive names in the shared catalogue now exist in both languages, so a
cardboard spool reads as "Kartonspule (ab 2021)" in German and "Cardboard
spool (from 2021)" in English. Manufacturers keep their name — "Polymaker"
reads the same everywhere.

Missing a translation is not a problem: the original name is shown instead.

Suggesting a spool for the catalogue now lets you supply both languages at
once, if you know them.

## A name and a face

The app is now called **filahub** everywhere — in the sidebar, on the sign-in
screen and in the browser tab — and it has a logo: a spool seen from the front,
with a fill arc.

## Smaller improvements

- The sidebar no longer shows a stray horizontal scrollbar above the footer.
- The bulk-import prompt is generated in your language.
- Currency and regional-format names in the settings appear in your language.
