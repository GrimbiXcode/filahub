## Settings moved into the sidebar

The settings used to be reachable only by typing the address by hand. They now
sit at the bottom of the sidebar, right above your profile.

## Signing in without Telegram while developing

Running the app locally no longer requires a Telegram bot. With `DEV_LOGIN=1`
the login page offers a shortcut that creates a local test account. It has no
effect in production.
