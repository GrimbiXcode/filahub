---

## Add photos to a print

Every print now has a **“Photos and files”** section. Add photos from your
computer, drag them onto the page, or – on your phone – take one straight
from the camera. The first photo becomes the cover and shows up in the print
list, so you recognise a print at a glance; any other photo can be made the
cover instead. Tap a photo to see it large and flip through the others.

Photos are made smaller before they leave your device, and everything a
camera writes into a photo besides the picture – **including where it was
taken** – is removed. A photo of your printer does not reveal your address.

## Keep the 3MF project with the print

Attach the 3MF file you printed from, so the exact project with its settings
is right there the next time you want to print it again. 3MF files are kept
as they are and can be downloaded from the print's page.

## Download your files

Under **Settings → Data and account**, a new button downloads all your photos
and 3MF files as one ZIP, next to the existing data export.

Each print holds up to 20 files, and each account up to 1 GB. Photos and files
stay private: friends you share a store with do not see them. In an
organization, everyone who can log prints can add photos, and anyone who can
edit materials can delete them.
