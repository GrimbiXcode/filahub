## "Pla" is "PLA"

Typing a material type in lower case, or with a stray capital, used to create
a second material type. From then on the suggestions offered "PLA" and "Pla"
side by side, and the overview filter listed both as if they were different
materials.

Material types no longer care about upper and lower case. Type "pla" and the
app uses "PLA"; type "petg" and it becomes "PETG". A type that is not on the
suggestion list keeps the spelling you gave it the first time, and every later
entry of the same word takes that spelling, whichever keys you press. You see
it happen as soon as you leave the field, and the suggested name of the
material uses the same spelling.

The same applies when you change a material later and when you bring in a
whole order at once.

## What happened to the duplicates you already had

If your stock had "PLA" and "Pla" next to each other, they have been merged.
The spelling from the suggestion list wins; for anything else, the spelling you
used most often – and if it was a tie, the one you used first.
