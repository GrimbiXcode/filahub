## New

Prints from the printer bridge now arrive in your inbox with their preview
picture – the same small image your printer shows for the file. You can see
at a glance which print is which before you take it over.

When you take over a print, the preview becomes its first picture and its
cover in the print history. You can still add your own photos and choose
another cover at any time. If you discard the entry, the preview goes with
it.

The preview comes straight from the print file; nothing else from the file is
kept with it. Prints whose file has no preview arrive as before.

Previews count towards the storage space for your photos and projects.

Update your printer bridge once (fetch its new version and start it again).
