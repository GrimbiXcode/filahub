## The catalogue knows 27 manufacturers

It used to know four. Alongside Polymaker, Prusament, Bambu Lab and eSUN, the
shared catalogue now carries SUNLU, Overture, ELEGOO, Anycubic, Creality,
HATCHBOX, Extrudr, Fillamentum, FormFutura, Spectrum, Devil Design, Das
Filament, 3DJAKE, add:north, AzureFilm, Rosa3D, JAYO, FlashForge, Eryone,
Amazon Basics, TINMORRY, GEEETECH and QIDI Tech — 76 spool sizes in total.

The four that were already there grew as well. Polymaker gained the cardboard
spool that PolyLite switched to, plus PolyMax, PolySonic, PolyFlex and Fiberon.
Prusament is no longer just PLA: PETG, ASA, PVB and TPU are listed with their
own sizes, including the 2 kg spool. eSUN gained the cardboard spool its PLA
lines ship on today, and Bambu Lab the 500 g size.

Nothing you changed yourself is touched. Entries an administrator edited, and
suggestions that were accepted from the community, keep their values — only
filahub's own starter entries are brought up to date.

A word of caution that has not changed: manufacturers redesign spools without
announcing it, and cardboard takes on water. Treat every figure as a starting
point, not as the spool in your hand. If it matters, weigh the empty spool once
and copy the preset into a spool type of your own.

## filahub on your home screen

filahub now has a proper app icon. Add it to the home screen of your phone or
to the dock on your desktop and you get the spool logo instead of a blurred
screenshot of the page — and the app opens without a browser address bar in the
way.

## A button that shows you how

Under **Settings → Quick access** there is now a button for exactly that. Where
your browser offers to install a web app, the button asks it to. Where it does
not — on an iPhone, for instance, Safari has no such thing — the button
explains the two taps it takes instead, for the browser you happen to be using.
