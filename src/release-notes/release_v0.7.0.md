## Release notes in the app

You are looking at the new feature: whenever an update brings something new,
the sidebar shows how many entries you have not read yet. Open **Neuerungen**
to catch up — entries you have already seen lose their marker, across every
device you sign in on.

![The release notes page with four unread entries, the counter next to Neuerungen in the sidebar](images/v0.7.0-release-notes.png)

## Dark theme

The app now comes in light and dark, and by default it follows your operating
system. Switch it in the settings or with the toggle in the header on a phone.
The choice is stored per device, so your phone may stay dark while your desktop
stays light.

## Built for the phone

- Tables with many columns turn into readable cards instead of scrolling
  sideways.
- The sidebar collapses to icons and slides in as an overlay on narrow screens.
- A slim header keeps weighing, searching and the theme toggle one tap away.

## Quick actions everywhere

Weighing a spool, adding a material and the quick search no longer depend on
which page you are on:

- **Weigh** and **Add material** sit at the top of the sidebar.
- Press `Ctrl` + `K` (`⌘` + `K` on a Mac) to search from anywhere.
