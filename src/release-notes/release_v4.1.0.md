title: Print settings and used-up spools
---

## Print settings for each material

Every material now has a place for its print settings: nozzle and bed
temperature, chamber, fan, speed, flow, retraction and how to dry it – or, for
resin, the exposure times, bottom layers, layer height and post-cure. Add them
on the material's page under **“Print settings”**. Only fill in what you know;
empty fields stay empty.

There is room for notes as well, with simple formatting such as **bold** text
and lists – for everything that does not fit into a number, like “glue stick
on glass” or the name of your slicer profile.

The settings belong to the material, so they apply to every spool of it. The
most important values – nozzle, bed and drying – also appear as a short line
next to the shelf and on each spool's page, where you look them up while
standing at the printer.

Your print settings stay private. Friends you share a store with do not see
them.

## Mark a spool as used up

An empty spool no longer has to be deleted. On the spool's page, **“Used up”**
takes it off the shelf and out of the stock count, but keeps its history, and
the material with its print settings stays. If you change your mind, **“Back
in use”** brings it back.

Used-up spools are listed under “Spools of this material”, folded away below
the others. When you try to delete a spool, filahub now offers to mark it as
used up instead.

Used-up spools no longer appear for friends you share a store with, and they
are left out when you weigh or log usage. The quick search still finds them,
and typing a used-up spool's identifier opens its page.

When every spool of a material is used up, the material counts as out of
stock: its page says so, and the overview shows a warning with a link to it –
that is the moment to buy more.

When you merge two materials, or move a material's last spool to another
material, the print settings of the material you move to are kept. If it has
none, it takes over the others.
