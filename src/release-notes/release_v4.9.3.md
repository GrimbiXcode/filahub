## Improved

- **No more cut-off pages.** Long print titles, file names, tags and names of
  storages, dryboxes, printers or bridges no longer push pages past the right
  edge of the screen. They wrap onto the next line instead – on the print
  details, the print list, material and container pages, the inbox and
  everywhere else.
- **Navigation shows when there is more.** If the menu doesn't fit on your
  screen, the list now fades out at the bottom with a shadow above the
  footer, so you can tell there are more entries to scroll to.
- **Logging a print on the phone:** each material row puts the material on
  its own line, with grams below, so you can read which spool you picked.
- **Connections:** the printer bridge card is more compact on the phone. Rename,
  revoke, edit and remove are icon buttons, and the bridge name gets the full
  width.
